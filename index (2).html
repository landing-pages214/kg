About Kyle

A personal lab for real software.

Kyle Goertzen builds his own projects and uses each one as a place to explore how software can work as a complete product. The work moves between user-facing experiences and the infrastructure that makes those experiences possible.

Rather than presenting a long list of unrelated work, this site keeps the focus on Kyle and the systems he has actually created. The centerpiece is Kyle20000, alongside the broader technical thinking that goes into building independent digital products.

Systems Mindset

Think in layers.

Modern software is more than a screen. Kyle's work looks at the connection between the interface a person sees and the application behavior, data and backend systems underneath it.

That systems-first perspective is especially visible in Kyle20000, where the product is centered on a server-controlled ledger and the interactions around it.

01 / EXPERIENCE

Interface

The visual layer that gives a digital system a clear, usable front end.

02 / LOGIC

Application

The rules and behavior that connect actions to outcomes.

03 / DATA

Records

Persistent information that lets an application remember its state.

04 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Backend

The server-side foundation that makes the overall system operate.

The Approach

Make the
idea real.

For Kyle Goertzen, building software is about moving from “what if?” to “here it is.” A project becomes more interesting when it stops being an idea and becomes something that can be opened, tested, improved and used.

That means thinking about both sides of the product: what the user experiences and what the system has to do behind the scenes. The goal is not simply to write code. It is to create a complete working thing.
